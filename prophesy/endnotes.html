  • 41.Humphreys, Colin J., and W.G. Waddington. "Dating the Crucifixion." Nature 306 (December 22/29, 1983): 743–746. Peer-reviewed astronomical study confirming a partial lunar eclipse (approximately 59% magnitude at maximum) at moonrise on Friday, April 3, AD 33 — Passover, Nisan 14. Establishes AD 33 as the only date in the viable crucifixion range satisfying a Friday Nisan 14 with a concurrent lunar eclipse. Counter-argument: NASA scientist Bradley Schaefer (Quarterly Journal of the Royal Astronomical Society 31 [1990]: 53–67) concludes the eclipse at ~59% partial would have been visually unimpressive at the Jerusalem horizon. Sigismondi (2012) disputes Schaefer’s atmospheric calculation. The eclipse is confirmed; the visual blood-moon appearance is disputed. Note: this eclipse does NOT fulfill Joel 2:31 — that passage refers to Day of the LORD signs in the Tribulation period (Walvoord, Ryrie, Pentecost). It is a historically confirmed astronomical event coinciding with the date of the crucifixion.
