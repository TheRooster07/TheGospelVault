33 Truths at Salvation for Every Believer

Sealed and Filled — the Spirit’s Firstfruits

Key Verse: Ephesians 1:14  ·  NASB1995

Key Verse

“who is given as a pledge of our inheritance, with a view to the redemption of God’s own possession, to the praise of His glory.”

Ephesians 1:14

The Spirit’s ministry to believers is both a present possession and a guarantee of more to come — like a down payment on a promise God fully intends to keep.

What This Means for You

I.

Born of the Spirit

“That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.”

John 3:6 · NASB1995

Your new spiritual life didn’t originate with you. Jesus contrasts the two births directly: “that which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit” (John 3:6) — a life your natural birth never could have produced, because it was the Spirit’s own work.

II.

Baptized with the Spirit

“For by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, and we were all made to drink of one Spirit.”

1 Corinthians 12:13 · NASB1995

The moment you believed, the Spirit placed you into the body of Christ. First Corinthians 12:13 says “by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body” — a single, unrepeatable act joining you permanently to every other believer, regardless of background.

III.

Indwelt by the Spirit

“Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God, and that you are not your own?”

1 Corinthians 6:19 · NASB1995

“Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we may know the things freely given to us by God,”

1 Corinthians 2:12 · NASB1995

“But this He spoke of the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were to receive; for the Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.”

John 7:39 · NASB1995

“and hope does not disappoint, because the love of God has been poured out within our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us”

Romans 5:5 · NASB1995

“However, you are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you.”

Romans 8:9 · NASB1995

“Now He who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,”

2 Corinthians 1:21 · NASB1995

“Because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, 'Abba! Father!'”

Galatians 4:6 · NASB1995

“The one who keeps His commandments abides in Him, and He in him. We know by this that He abides in us, by the Spirit whom He has given us.”

1 John 3:24 · NASB1995

Your body is described, remarkably, as a temple: “your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you” (1 Cor. 6:19). First Corinthians 2:12 says this same Spirit lets you “know the things freely given to us by God,” and John 7:39 marks this indwelling as something that began only once Jesus was glorified. Romans 5:5 traces God’s love “poured out within our hearts through the Holy Spirit,” Romans 8:9 makes indwelling the very evidence of belonging to Christ, and 2 Corinthians 1:21 and Galatians 4:6 both describe the Spirit crying “Abba! Father!” within you. First John 3:24 adds the confirming sign: “we know...He abides in us, by the Spirit whom He has given us.”

IV.

Sealed with the Spirit

“Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.”

Ephesians 4:30 · NASB1995

“who also sealed us and gave us the Spirit in our hearts as a pledge.”

2 Corinthians 1:22 · NASB1995

A seal marked ownership and security in the ancient world. Ephesians 4:30 warns against grieving “the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption,” and 2 Corinthians 1:22 says God “sealed us and gave us the Spirit in our hearts as a pledge.” The Spirit’s presence in you is God’s own guarantee that you belong to Him until the day of redemption.

V.

Anointed with the Spirit

“Now He who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,”

2 Corinthians 1:21 · NASB1995

“But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know.”

1 John 2:20 · NASB1995

Just as prophets, priests, and kings were once anointed for their calling, 2 Corinthians 1:21 says God Himself “anointed us,” and 1 John 2:20 tells believers, “you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know” — every believer is anointed by the Spirit, equipped, not left to serve God under their own strength.

Reflect

  1. Which of these five works of the Spirit — new birth, baptism, indwelling, sealing, or anointing — did you know least about before this study?
  2. Does it change how you approach temptation or fear to remember the Spirit is not just helping you from outside, but permanently living inside you?
