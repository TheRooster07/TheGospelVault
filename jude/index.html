The Vault — Devotional Series

Jude

A 6-part devotional companion to Jude — called, beloved, and kept for Jesus Christ, contending for the faith once for all handed down, and resting in the One who is able to keep you from stumbling.

  1. 01 Standing in the Storm: Grace, Mercy, and Peace Jude 1–2 Before a word about the storm, Jude tells you who you are in it — called, beloved, and kept for Jesus Christ. →
  2. 02 The Battle Cry: Contend Earnestly for the Faith Jude 3–4 Jude meant to write about our common salvation; instead he calls the beloved to contend for a faith handed down once for all. →
  3. 03 Three Warnings from History: When Grace Is Misused Jude 5–11 Israel, the angels, Sodom, and three named men — history’s warnings, and a God whose judging and keeping are equally consistent. →
  4. 04 Portraits of Apostasy: What False Teachers Look Like Jude 12–16 Waterless clouds, wandering stars, uprooted trees — the test of a teacher is not the presentation but what remains. →
  5. 05 The Believer’s Response: Build, Pray, Keep, Rescue Jude 17–23 “But you, beloved” — build, pray, keep, and wait, then reach out with mercy to those in danger. →
  6. 06 The Doxology of Confidence: He Is Able Jude 24–25 The letter ends not with one more command but with praise to the One who is able to keep you from stumbling. →
