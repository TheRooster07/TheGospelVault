New Testament Book Introductions

All 27 books, one page each: who wrote it, when, what it is about, a short outline and key verses. Read them in Bible order, or switch to the order they were written and follow the story on the New Testament Timeline.

Gospels and Acts

The life of Christ and the birth and spread of His church.

About AD 50MatthewJesus is the promised King, the Son of David. About AD 65–67MarkJesus the Servant, who came to give His life. About AD 60LukeThe Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost. Paul’s Letters

Paul wrote all thirteen. In order of writing: Galatians, 1–2 Thessalonians, 1–2 Corinthians, Romans, the prison letters (Ephesians, Colossians, Philemon, Philippians), then 1 Timothy, Titus and 2 Timothy.
