Vault 6

Resource Links

A curated collection of outside teaching, commentaries, and study tools worth your time — for going deeper than what's found here alone.

Available Now

Featured Resources

Research Guide

Research Resource Guide

Large-scale, publicly available data sources for foreign missions planning — Bible translation status, unreached people groups, religious persecution, digital connectivity, Deaf ministry, and general demographics.

 Study Guide

Apologetics & Study Resource Guide

Trusted commentary, apologetics, creationism, and biblical archaeology resources — selected for conservative, evangelical reliability.
Free Grace

Free Grace Study Library

Ministries and schools that teach eternal life as a free gift received by faith alone in Christ alone — the same foundation as our Statement of Faith. Most offer free articles, journals, and books.

Journal & Books

Grace Evangelical Society

Blog, the Journal of the Grace Evangelical Society, Grace in Focus magazine, and free ebooks by Zane Hodges, Bob Wilkin, and others — several in other languages.

 Short Studies

GraceLife Ministries — GraceNotes

Dr. Charlie Bing's concise studies on salvation, assurance, and the Christian life, plus Bible outlines and free translations.

 Education

Grace School of Theology

A Free Grace seminary offering courses and degrees, with Grace Theology Press publishing on salvation and rewards.

 Education

Chafer Theological Seminary

Grace-oriented, dispensational training, and the Pneumatikos journal.

 Teaching

Two Circles Ministries

Dr. Kenny Ray Hodges's teaching on the difference between receiving eternal life and following Christ as a disciple.

 Classic Studies

Biblical Research Society

Dr. David L. Cooper's studies on the Messiah, prophecy, and Israel.
Israel & Prophecy

Israel, Messiah & Prophecy

Dispensational, premillennial resources on the Messiah in the Old Testament, the feasts, Israel's future, and the return of Christ.

Messianic Studies

Ariel Ministries

Dr. Arnold Fruchtenbaum's teaching on messianic prophecy, Israelology, and the life of the Messiah from a Jewish perspective.

 Israel

The Friends of Israel

Ministry to the Jewish people and teaching on Israel and prophecy; publishers of Israel My Glory.

 Prophecy

Pre-Trib Research Center

Articles and conference papers defending the pretribulational rapture and premillennial prophecy.

 Jewish Background

Chosen People Ministries

Jewish-background teaching on the feasts, Jewish customs, and Jesus as Israel's Messiah.
Every Language

Scripture in Other Languages & Study Tools

Free Bibles and study tools for reading Scripture in many languages and in the original Greek and Hebrew — helpful for sharing the Gospel with friends and neighbors who speak another language.

Bibles

eBible.org

Free, openly licensed and public-domain Bible translations in more than a thousand languages, for reading, download, and printing.

 Original Languages

STEP Bible

Free Greek and Hebrew study tools from Tyndale House, Cambridge — word studies, interlinear text, and many translations.

 Classic Books

Christian Classics Ethereal Library

Free classic Christian books in the public domain, including older Bible commentaries and theology.

 Archaeology

BiblePlaces.com

Photographs and descriptions of the places of the Bible across Israel, Jordan, Egypt, and beyond.
Context & Credibility

Research & Trends Resources

Respected research organizations cited for cultural and statistical context in these studies — not doctrinal sources, but useful for grounding a topical study or white paper in current data on faith, church trends, and global Christianity.

Digital & U.S. Trends

Barna Group

Long-running evangelical research on American faith, church attendance, generational belief, and — increasingly — digital/media engagement with faith content.

 Global

Pew Research Center — Religion

The leading non-partisan source for global religious demographics, including the Global Religious Landscape and Global Religious Futures data by country.

 Global

Center for the Study of Global Christianity

Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary's World Christian Database and annual Status of Global Christianity report — the academic standard for worldwide Christian population data.

 Global

Operation World

Country-by-country prayer and missions profiles with religious demographic breakdowns, long trusted in evangelical mission circles.

 Global

Lausanne Movement

Global evangelical research network; publishes the State of the Great Commission report with regional breakdowns across Europe, Asia, and beyond.

 Europe

Evangelical Alliance (UK)

Annual "Missional Trends" research series — practical, church-health-focused survey data for the UK and broader European context.

 Europe

European Evangelical Alliance

Continent-wide evangelical umbrella body and network hub for country-level European evangelical data and contacts.

 Asia

Pew Research — Asia Religion Studies

Country-level religion data for East and South Asia, including religious-switching research on Korea, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.

 Asia-Pacific

National Church Life Survey

A respected, long-running church-health survey covering Australia, New Zealand, and the wider Asia-Pacific region.

 Asia

Asia Evangelical Alliance

Regional network body for evangelical churches across Asia — a hub for locating country-specific research contacts.
A Note on Outside Resources

Use with discernment

These are independent ministries and sites. Listing one here reflects its usefulness for study, not agreement with every position it holds. On the Gospel itself, our position is set out in the Statement of Faith. Know a resource we should add? Let us know.
