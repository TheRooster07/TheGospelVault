Hard Questions · Part IV, Question 7

Is Baptism Necessary for Salvation?

Translation: NKJV · Free Grace / Dispensational · Source: The Gospel Vault

In Brief

No. Baptism is important and commanded, but it is not the condition for eternal life. Forgiveness and the Holy Spirit are given to everyone who believes, before baptism (Acts 10:43–44). Baptism is the public response to forgiveness already received.

Key versesActs 10:43–44 · Acts 2:38 · Romans 4:5

Faith alone, in every age. Abraham “believed in the LORD, and He accounted it to him for righteousness” (Genesis 15:6), years before he was circumcised (Romans 4:10–11). If justification came before the sign under the old covenant, no rite can be the condition of it under the new. God justifies “the ungodly” who believe (Romans 4:5).

Cornelius settles it. Peter told Cornelius’s household, “whoever believes in Him will receive remission of sins” (Acts 10:43). “While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all those who heard the word” (10:44). They were forgiven and received the Spirit before anyone was baptized. Their baptism followed (10:47–48).

What about Acts 2:38? “Repent, and let every one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins.” In the Greek, “repent” is plural and is tied directly to “remission of sins”; “be baptized” changes to the singular, setting it apart (Stanley Toussaint). And the word “for” (eis) can mean “because of” (Daniel Wallace notes this use is real, not merely possible). So baptism follows forgiveness; it does not obtain it.

“Baptism now saves us” (1 Peter 3:21). Peter adds his own clarification: “not the removal of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God), through the resurrection of Jesus Christ.” The water does not save; the risen Christ, received by faith, does.

Why it matters. Baptism is a joyful act of obedience for every believer, but it is a consequence of salvation, not a condition of it. Whoever believes in Jesus has eternal life now (John 5:24).
