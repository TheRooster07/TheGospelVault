The Man Born Blind

He Is the Light of the World  ·  John 9  ·  The Seven Signs

Key Verse

“One thing I do know, that though I was blind, now I see.”

John 9:25

The disciples saw a theological puzzle: who sinned to cause this? Jesus saw an opportunity for “the works of God” to be displayed. He made clay, sent the man to wash in Siloam, and the man came back seeing. The rest of the chapter is an interrogation. Neighbors doubt, parents hedge, and Pharisees badger, but the man’s understanding keeps growing: “the man who is called Jesus,” then “He is a prophet,” then finally, “Lord, I believe,” as he worships Him. He couldn’t win a debate with the scholars, and he didn’t need to. His testimony was simple and unshakable: I was blind, now I see. The Light of the World doesn’t wait for us to understand everything before He opens our eyes.

A Question to Carry

  1. Can you state your own “one thing I do know” about what Christ has done for you as simply as this man did?
