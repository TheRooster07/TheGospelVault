Who is Jesus?

The Bible was written so that you may know who Jesus is and have life in Him:

but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.

John 20:31 (WEB)

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 3:16 (WEB)

Eternal life is a free gift. It is not earned by good works or kept by our effort. It is received the moment a person believes in Jesus for it, and God promises that those who believe can know they have it:

These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, that you may know that you have eternal life, and that you may continue to believe in the name of the Son of God.

1 John 5:13 (WEB)

Your privacy and safety

Locked before it leaves your device. Your question is encrypted on your own phone or computer before it is sent, then sent through a secure connection. Only The Gospel Vault's private answer service can unlock it.

Nothing that identifies you is kept. We never keep your name, location, internet address, device details, or the time you asked. Phone numbers and email addresses typed into a question are removed before it is saved. This page uses no cookies, tracking, or outside services.

Your words are not kept online. Your question waits in protected storage only until The Gospel Vault downloads it to answer it, and is then deleted from the service. Questions are never posted, and any answer we publish is written in our own words so no one could be identified.

Your reference number is yours alone. It is random and is not linked to you in any way. We cannot look it up for you, so please write it down.

What no website can hide: someone who watches your internet connection may still see that you visited this website, though not what you asked. If that could put you in danger, use the Tor Browser, and think about who may see your device or your notes. A private (incognito) browser window keeps this visit out of your history. The Quick exit button (or pressing Esc twice) leaves this page at once. Links to Bible websites open other sites, which have their own privacy rules.

Every answer on this page is written and reviewed by The Gospel Vault. Always read the passages for yourself and test every answer by Scripture (Acts 17:11).

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (WEB), which is in the public domain.
