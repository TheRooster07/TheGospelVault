A note on outside resources

The Gospel Vault recommends these resources for Bible study, research, and spiritual growth. Some are written from the same Free Grace and dispensational view held here. Others are listed for a specific strength, such as history, archaeology, language study, apologetics, or creation science, even though their teaching on other subjects may differ from ours. Listing a resource is not an endorsement of everything it teaches.

On how a person receives eternal life, our position is stated in our Statement of Faith: eternal life is God's free gift, received by faith alone in Jesus Christ alone. Like the Bereans, test everything you read by Scripture (Acts 17:11).
