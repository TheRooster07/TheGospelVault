Vault 3 · New Testament Book Introductions · Paul’s Letters

Titus

Sound doctrine that produces good works.

Author
Paul
Date
AD 62–63, between his imprisonments
Key verses
Titus 1:5; 3:5; 3:8

For this reason I left you in Crete, that you would set in order what remains and appoint elders in every city as I directed you…

— Titus 1:5 (NASB95)

Theme

Sound doctrine that produces good works. Paul tells Titus to appoint elders in Crete, teach each group in the church to live in a way that adorns the gospel, and remind those who have believed to be careful to do good.

Outline

  1. Greeting (1:1–4)
  2. Officers in the church: qualified elders (1:5–9)
  3. Offenders in the church: silencing false teachers (1:10–16)
  4. Order in the church: sound living for each group, the grace that teaches us, good works in society (2:1–3:11)
  5. Closing (3:12–15)

More key verses

He saved us, not on the basis of deeds which we have done in righteousness, but according to His mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewing by the Holy Spirit…

— Titus 3:5 (NASB95)

This is a trustworthy statement; and concerning these things I want you to speak confidently, so that those who have believed God will be careful to engage in good deeds. These things are good and profitable for men.

— Titus 3:8 (NASB95)
