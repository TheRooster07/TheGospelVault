Devotional Series

1 Thessalonians

A 10-part devotional companion to Paul’s first letter to the Thessalonians — a young church born under persecution, and the faith, love, and hope that carried it, passage by passage.

  1. 01 An Introduction to the Thessalonian Church 1 Thessalonians 1:1–5 Paul gives thanks not for their activity, but for its source — a work of faith, labor of love, and patience of hope, all rooted in being beloved and chosen by God. →
  2. 02 The Many Faces of the Gospel 1 Thessalonians 1:6–10 A church born in weeks, not years, becomes a pattern worth imitating — joy inside affliction, and a testimony that outran Paul himself. →
  3. 03 Delivering the Gospel 1 Thessalonians 2:1–12 Paul preached with fresh scars from Philippi still on his back — faithfulness was his assignment, and the outcome was always God's to give. →
  4. 04 A Successful Life of Service 1 Thessalonians 2:13–20 Paul's crown was never a title or an achievement — it was people, the living proof that a life poured out for others actually counted. →
  5. 05 Facing Tribulation 1 Thessalonians 3:1–13 Unable to bear not knowing how his young converts were holding up, Paul sends Timothy — and their standing fast becomes his own reason to keep going. →
  6. 06 A Life That Pleases God 1 Thessalonians 4:1–12 Not a new rulebook, but an outworking — a quiet, steady life that grows from what God has already placed inside a believer. →
  7. 07 The Rapture of the Church 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18 Grief with hope, not without it — a promised reunion in the air, and believers who will always, not just eventually, be with the Lord. →
  8. 08 The Coming of the Lord: A Purifying Hope 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11 Not appointed to wrath but to salvation — a certainty meant to produce watchfulness, not fear, and comfort worth sharing. →
  9. 09 The Coming of the Lord: Exhortations 1 Thessalonians 5:12–22 God's will in three short commands — rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in everything — not conditional on mood or circumstance. →
  10. 10 ’Til We Meet Again 1 Thessalonians 5:23–28 A letter that opened in grace closes in grace — and the One who calls us to complete sanctification is faithful enough to finish it. →
