Vault 3 · NT Book Studies

Jude

A six-part small group Bible study series through Jude's urgent little letter — contending for the faith once for all handed down, and trusting the God who is able to keep you from stumbling. Each study includes the biblical text, a devotional, a full commentary, a self-study guide with Greek word study, and personal application.

Six Studies

Choose a Passage

  1. 01Standing in the Storm: Grace, Mercy, and PeaceJude 1–2Before a word of warning, Jude tells his readers who they are — called, beloved, and kept for Jesus Christ — and prays for mercy, peace, and love to overflow.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
  2. 02The Battle Cry: Contend Earnestly for the FaithJude 3–4Jude meant to write about “our common salvation” and was forced to write a call to contend — because the faith once for all handed down was being turned into license.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
  3. 03Three Warnings from History: When Grace Is MisusedJude 5–11A redeemed generation destroyed, angels in chains, Sodom as an example — and Cain, Balaam, and Korah: God has judged this pattern before.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
  4. 04Portraits of Apostasy: What False Teachers Look LikeJude 12–16Hidden reefs, waterless clouds, uprooted trees, wild waves, wandering stars — and Enoch’s ancient verdict. Jude teaches his readers to recognize what bears no fruit.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
  5. 05The Believer’s Response: Build, Pray, Keep, RescueJude 17–23“But you, beloved…” After the warnings, Jude’s prescription: build, pray, keep yourselves in God’s love, wait for Christ’s mercy — and reach out with three different kinds of mercy.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
  6. 06The Doxology of Confidence: He Is AbleJude 24–25Jude’s letter of warnings ends in worship: God is able to keep you from stumbling and to make you stand before His glory blameless with great joy.→Leader / Learner GuideOne page · PDF download
