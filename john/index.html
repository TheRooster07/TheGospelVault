The Vault — Devotional Series

John: An Eyewitness Testimony

Twenty-one chapters, twenty-one days — a verse-by-verse walk through the Gospel of John. Each chapter includes a key verse, a short reflection, and questions to carry with you. Start at Chapter 1, or jump to whichever chapter you're studying.

  1. 01The Word Became FleshJohn 1:1–51The eternal Word steps into human history — full of grace and truth.→
  2. 02Water Into Wine, Zeal for His Father's HouseJohn 2:1–25The first sign at Cana, and a temple cleared for true worship.→
  3. 03Born Again, and the Love That Sent HimJohn 3:1–36Nicodemus by night, and the love that gave the world its Son.→
  4. 04Living Water, and a Father's Growing FaithJohn 4:1–54A woman at a well, and a father whose faith grows step by step.→
  5. 05From Death Into LifeJohn 5:1–47Healing at the pool of Bethesda, and the authority behind it.→
  6. 06Bread from Heaven, Words of Eternal LifeJohn 6:1–71Five thousand fed, and a harder teaching that thins the crowd.→
  7. 07Rivers of Living WaterJohn 7:1–52A divided crowd, and an invitation to come and drink.→
  8. 08The Truth That Sets You FreeJohn 8:1–59Grace for the accused, and freedom found only in the Son.→
  9. 09I Was Blind, But Now I SeeJohn 9:1–41A man born blind, and the simple testimony no argument can shake.→
  10. 10No One Can Snatch Them Out of My HandJohn 10:1–42The Good Shepherd, and the eternal security of His sheep.→
  11. 11I Am the Resurrection and the LifeJohn 11:1–57Lazarus raised, and a question every reader has to answer.→
  12. 12A Grain of Wheat Must FallJohn 12:1–50The road to the cross comes into view, and what it costs to follow.→
  13. 13Loved to the EndJohn 13:1–38A towel, a basin, and a new commandment to love one another.→
  14. 14The Way, the Truth, and the LifeJohn 14:1–31A troubled heart calmed, and the only way to the Father.→
  15. 15Abiding in the VineJohn 15:1–27Fruitfulness that flows from staying connected to Christ.→
  16. 16I Have Overcome the WorldJohn 16:1–33Grief turned to joy, and peace that doesn't depend on circumstances.→
  17. 17The Prayer Jesus Prayed for YouJohn 17:1–26Jesus prays for His disciples — and for everyone who would believe through them.→
  18. 18Of Those You Gave Me, I Have Lost NoneJohn 18:1–40Betrayal, arrest, and a promise kept even in the garden.→
  19. 19It Is FinishedJohn 19:1–42The cross, and the completed work of redemption.→
  20. 20That You May BelieveJohn 20:1–31The empty tomb, and the reason John wrote this Gospel at all.→
  21. 21Feed My SheepJohn 21:1–25Breakfast on the shore, and Peter fully restored.→
