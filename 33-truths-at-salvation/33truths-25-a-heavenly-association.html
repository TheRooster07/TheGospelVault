33 Truths at Salvation for Every Believer

A Heavenly Association

Key Verse: Ephesians 2:6  ·  NASB1995

Key Verse

“and raised us up with Him, and seated us with Him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,”

Ephesians 2:6

God gives believers a real share in Christ’s own life, position, work, and even suffering — a genuine partnership, not a one-sided arrangement.

What This Means for You

I.

Partners with Christ in Life

“When Christ, who is our life, is revealed, then you also will be revealed with Him in glory.”

Colossians 3:4 · NASB1995

“And the testimony is this, that God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son. He who has the Son has the life; he who does not have the Son of God does not have the life.”

1 John 5:11-12 · NASB1995

Christ’s own life is now literally your life. Colossians 3:4 calls Him “our life” outright, and 1 John 5:11-12 makes the connection unbreakable: “He who has the Son has the life.” Where He is, spiritually, you already are as well.

II.

Partners with Christ in Position

“and raised us up with Him, and seated us with Him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,”

Ephesians 2:6 · NASB1995

God has already seated believers with Christ in the heavenly realms. Ephesians 2:6 says He “raised us up with Him, and seated us with Him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus” — language written entirely in the past tense. This is a present reality, not just a future hope.

III.

Partners with Christ in Service

“God is faithful, through whom you were called into fellowship with His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.”

1 Corinthians 1:9 · NASB1995

“For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's field, God's building.”

1 Corinthians 3:9 · NASB1995

“And working together with Him, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain—”

2 Corinthians 6:1 · NASB1995

“Therefore, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making an appeal through us; we beg you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God.”

2 Corinthians 5:20 · NASB1995

“but in everything commending ourselves as servants of God, in much endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,”

2 Corinthians 6:4 · NASB1995

“being manifested that you are a letter of Christ, cared for by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts.”

2 Corinthians 3:3 · NASB1995

“who also made us adequate as servants of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit; for the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.”

2 Corinthians 3:6 · NASB1995

Whatever work God gives you to do, you do it as a genuine co-laborer with Christ. First Corinthians 1:9 says you were “called into fellowship with His Son,” and 1 Corinthians 3:9 states it plainly: “we are God’s fellow workers.” Second Corinthians 6:1 pictures believers “working together with Him,” 2 Corinthians 5:20 sends you out as “ambassadors for Christ,” and 2 Corinthians 6:4 calls you “servants of God” even through hardship. Second Corinthians 3:3,6 adds that you become, in effect, “a letter of Christ...written...with the Spirit,” a living message carried by “servants of a new covenant.”

IV.

Partners with Christ in Suffering

“If we endure, we will also reign with Him; if we deny Him, He also will deny us;”

2 Timothy 2:12 · NASB1995

“For to you it has been granted for Christ's sake, not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer for His sake,”

Philippians 1:29 · NASB1995

“But if when you do what is right and suffer for it you patiently endure it, this finds favor with God.”

1 Peter 2:20 · NASB1995

“Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery ordeal among you, which comes upon you for your testing, as though some strange thing were happening to you; but to the degree that you share the sufferings of Christ, keep on rejoicing, so that also at the revelation of His glory you may rejoice with exultation.”

1 Peter 4:12-13 · NASB1995

“so that no one would be disturbed by these afflictions; for you yourselves know that we have been destined for this.”

1 Thessalonians 3:3 · NASB1995

“For I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that is to be revealed to us.”

Romans 8:18 · NASB1995

“Now I rejoice in my sufferings for your sake, and in my flesh I do my share on behalf of His body, which is the church, in filling up what is lacking in Christ's afflictions.”

Colossians 1:24 · NASB1995

Even hardship isn’t wasted or meaningless. Second Timothy 2:12 links endurance now to reigning with Him later, and Philippians 1:29 says suffering “for His sake” is granted to you alongside believing, as part of the same gift. First Peter 2:20 and 4:12-13 both reframe unjust suffering as something that “finds favor with God” and lets you “share the sufferings of Christ,” while 1 Thessalonians 3:3 says believers “have been destined for this.” Romans 8:18 puts it all in perspective — present suffering is “not worthy to be compared with the glory” to come — and Colossians 1:24 says Paul could even rejoice in filling up “what is lacking in Christ’s afflictions” on the church’s behalf.

Reflect

  1. Which kind of partnership with Christ — life, position, service, or suffering — feels most real to you right now?
