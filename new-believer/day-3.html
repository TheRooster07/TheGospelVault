Day 3 · Middle · Input (I)

See and know God's promises.

Psalm 119:105; 2 Timothy 3:16-17; Hebrews 4:12 (NASB)

Ps 119:105 Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. 2 Tim 3:16-17 All Scripture is inspired by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness; so that the man of God may be adequate, equipped for every good work. Heb 4:12 For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword, and piercing as far as the division of soul and spirit… and able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.
See and know God's promises in His Word.

Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path (Psalm 119:105). Notice the psalmist doesn't ask for a floodlight over the whole horizon — just a lamp for the next step. Scripture usually gives you what you need to see right now, not the whole plan at once.

Paul tells Timothy Scripture is more than a lamp — it's a whole toolkit. All Scripture is inspired by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness (2 Timothy 3:16). It's not decoration for your shelf; it's the tool that equips you for every good work (2 Timothy 3:17).

Hebrews goes further still: the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing as far as the division of soul and spirit… and able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart (Hebrews 4:12). This isn't information to file away. It reaches places you can't reach on your own.

That's what it means to see and know God's promises — not just read words on a page, but let them show you who God is and who you're becoming.

For reflection

  1. Where in your life right now do you most need light for the next step, not the whole path?
  2. What's one small habit that could make opening Scripture something you do daily, not occasionally?

Feed on it today. Even a few verses let you see and know.
