The Vault — Devotional Series

Colossians

A 12-part devotional companion to Paul’s letter to the Colossians — Christ is all, and in Him you have been made complete, passage by passage.

  1. 01Faith, Love, and Hope in ColossaeColossians 1:1–8Writing in chains to a church he had never seen, Paul gives thanks for faith, love, and hope at work in Colossae.→
  2. 02Paul’s Prayer for the ColossiansColossians 1:9–14Paul’s prayer is built like a house: knowledge for a foundation, a worthy walk, and power to endure.→
  3. 03The Savior of the GospelColossians 1:15–23Christ is the image of the invisible God, Creator of all things, and the One who reconciled us through His cross.→
  4. 04Christ in You, the Hope of GloryColossians 1:24–29The secret the false teachers could never offer: Christ in you, the hope of glory.→
  5. 05The Believer’s EducationColossians 2:1–7You received Christ by grace through faith. Now walk in Him the same way, rooted, built up, and thankful.→
  6. 06Beware of PhilosophyColossians 2:8–15All the fullness of Deity dwells in Christ, and in Him you have been made complete.→
  7. 07Run the Race Set Before YouColossians 2:16–23Food laws and holy days were only shadows. The substance belongs to Christ.→
  8. 08Looking Up, Putting OffColossians 3:1–11Raised with Christ, believers are called to bring their practice up to their position.→
  9. 09Service Manual for the Lord’s ArmyColossians 3:12–17Chosen, holy, and beloved, believers put on the new uniform, and love holds it all together.→
  10. 10The Relationships of the New ManColossians 3:18–4:1The new life shows up at home and at work, where every relationship becomes service to the Lord.→
  11. 11Perfecting the Prayer LifeColossians 4:2–6Devoted prayer, a wise walk, and gracious speech toward those outside the faith.→
  12. 12Portrait of a Ministry TeamColossians 4:7–18Paul’s closing greetings reveal a ministry team of ordinary people, each with a place.→
