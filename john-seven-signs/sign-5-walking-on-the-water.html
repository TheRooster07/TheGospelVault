Walking on the Water

He Is the I AM  ·  John 6  ·  The Seven Signs

Key Verse

“But He said to them, ‘It is I; do not be afraid.’”

John 6:20

It’s dark, the wind is strong, and the disciples have rowed three or four miles against the sea. Then they see Jesus walking on the water toward them, and they are terrified. His words in Greek are two simple ones: egō eimi, “I am.” It’s the same expression God used when He revealed Himself to Moses, and the same one Jesus will claim outright in 8:58. The One who comes across the waves is the One who made them. Notice what happens next: the moment they receive Him into the boat, they are immediately at the land they were heading for. The storm didn’t define their trip; His presence did. Fear makes the storm look larger than the Lord. The answer isn’t stronger rowing but the One who says, “It is I.”

A Question to Carry

  1. What storm are you rowing against right now, and what would it look like to receive Him into the boat?
