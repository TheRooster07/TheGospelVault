Vault 3 · New Testament

Philemon

A three-part small group Bible study series through Paul’s shortest letter — a runaway slave, a wronged master, and an apostle who could have commanded but appealed for love’s sake: “Receive him as you would me.” Each study includes the biblical text, a devotional, a full commentary, a self-study guide with Greek word study, and personal application.

Source: Dr. C Norman Sellers ThD sellersbibleinstitute.com (used by permission)

For Leaders · Charts Three chart decks for leaders One deck for each study — the four G’s of Paul’s greeting, the three lines of his appeal, the ledger of debit and credit, and Mark’s “rest of the story” — PDF or PowerPoint, with leader notes on every slide.
