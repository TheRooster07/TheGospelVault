Colossians · Study 07 · Leader / Learner Guide

Colossians 2:16–23 — Leader / Learner Guide

Run the Race Set Before You · a one-page guide for leaders and learners

Christ Is All — A Study of Colossians  ·  TheGospelVault.com  ·  @iJohn.316 (Instagram)

Download PDF Full Study 07

Big Idea and Context

Big idea: Christ is the substance behind every shadow, so let no one judge you by rules or disqualify you through false spirituality; asceticism looks wise but has no power against the flesh.

Following "therefore" (2:16), Paul applies 2:8–15: since believers are complete in Christ and the debt is canceled, no one may impose Mosaic or ascetic rules on them.

  • The rules: food, drink, festival, new moon, Sabbath.
  • The prize: a believer's reward, which legalism can forfeit.
  • The verdict: "do not handle, taste, touch" has no value against the flesh.

Shadow and Substance — Three Anchor Verses

VerseWhat it saysTeaching point
2:16–17"no one is to act as your judge … a mere shadow of what is to come; but the substance belongs to Christ"Ceremonies were outlines; Christ is the body that casts them.
2:18–19"Let no one keep defrauding you of your prize … not holding fast to the head"The umpire picture: the issue is reward; the root error is letting go of Christ.
2:23"the appearance of wisdom … but are of no value against fleshly indulgence"Rules can look spiritual and still be powerless.

Four counterfeit "higher" paths: Gnosticism (knowledge), legalism (practice), mysticism (contemplation), asceticism (retreat).

Died with Christ (v.20): to sin, self, the Law, and the world.

Teaching Flow

  1. The areas of judging (2:16) — Food, drink, festival, new moon, Sabbath.
  2. Shadow and substance (2:17) — The ceremonies pointed to Christ.
  3. The prize at stake (2:18a) — A self-appointed referee; reward, not eternal life.
  4. The false alternative (2:18b–19) — False humility, angels, visions, pride, losing the Head.
  5. Asceticism exposed (2:20–23) — Dead to the world, why submit to rules? No value against the flesh.

Clarify While Teaching

  • "Prize": keep it distinct from eternal life; Reformed readers often take it as final salvation.
  • Sabbath: the study holds that it is not binding on the Church; present that firmly but without attacking individuals.
  • Food and conscience: freedom must be balanced with Romans 14; abstaining is not wrong, requiring it is.
  • Visions (v.18): the NASB95 reads "visions he has seen"; some manuscripts read "has not seen" (NKJV). Either way the claim rested on unverified experience.

Discussion and Close

  1. Has anyone ever made you feel less spiritual because of a rule not found in Scripture? Have you done that to others?
  2. What is the "prize" in verse 18, and how could a believer lose it?
  3. If rules cannot stop the flesh, what can?

Close: Next, Paul begins the practical half of the letter: seek the things above, where Christ is (3:1–11). "The substance belongs to Christ."
