The Vault — Devotional Series

John — 7 I AMs: Just Who Is Jesus?

Seven times in John’s Gospel, Jesus completes the sentence “I am…” with a picture His hearers already knew — bread, light, a door, a shepherd, resurrection, a way, a vine. Each one answers the same question: just who is Jesus? The series closes with the capstone claim underneath them all: “Before Abraham was, I AM.” Each devotional includes a key verse, a reflection, questions to carry, and the original Instagram slides.

  1. 01I Am the Bread of LifeJohn 6:35A crowd chasing loaves meets the Bread that ends hunger for good.→
  2. 02I Am the Light of the WorldJohn 8:12Under the temple’s great lamps, Jesus claims to be the light the whole world needs.→
  3. 03I Am the DoorJohn 10:9One way into the fold — and the Shepherd Himself lies across the gap.→
  4. 04I Am the Good ShepherdJohn 10:11A Shepherd who lays down His life — and never lets go of His sheep.→
  5. 05I Am the Resurrection and the LifeJohn 11:25–26Martha’s “someday” hope meets the One who is resurrection right now.→
  6. 06I Am the Way, the Truth, and the LifeJohn 14:6Thomas asks for directions; Jesus answers with Himself.→
  7. 07I Am the True VineJohn 15:5Fruit never comes from straining — only from staying joined to the Vine.→
  8. ★Before Abraham Was, I AMJohn 8:58  ·  CapstoneThe claim beneath all the others: Jesus is the eternal I AM.→
