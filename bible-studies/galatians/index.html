Vault 3 · NT Book Studies

Galatians

A sixteen-part small group Bible study series through Paul's letter to the churches of Galatia — the freedom found in Christ, and the danger of adding anything to the gospel of grace. Each study includes the biblical text, a devotional, a full commentary, a self-study guide with Greek word study, and personal application.
