Diaspora People Groups — Master List

17 unreached diaspora people groups, ranked by population, with each group's homeland, diaspora population, host-nation count, and reach into Freedom-House-rated Free nations.

Updated October 24, 2026 — Free-nation figures refreshed to Freedom House 2026; Pareto charts added.

At a Glance

Where the Diaspora Lives

Diaspora population by people group

Bars in millions, largest first; the line shows the running share of the 17-group total. The top five groups hold 48.1% of everyone on the list.

Diaspora living in Free host nations

The same groups re-ranked by how many members live in nations Freedom House rates Free, where open outreach is possible. About 15.04 million of 41.45 million (36.3%) live in Free nations, and the ranking changes sharply: Hindi, Gujarati and Japanese lead, while Uzbek, Hazara and Tajik fall to the bottom.

Free Pop and # Free are totals only, not a per-country breakdown — country-by-country population figures and individual openness ratings are intentionally left off this page for the safety of these communities. Full detail is kept for internal planning only.

Key

Columns

  • Grp — People group name (Joshua Project naming)
  • Nation¹ / Pop¹ — Homeland nation and its total population
  • Diaspora Pop² / # Nations² — Population living outside the homeland, and the number of host nations tracked
  • Free Pop³ / # Free³ — Of the diaspora above, the portion living in, and count of, host nations rated "Free" by Freedom House (aggregate only)

Sources

  • ¹ ² Joshua Project, people-group-in-country profiles, accessed August 2026. Afghan, general: seven host-nation figures updated in September 2026 to dated national-statistics estimates, because Joshua Project's figures for those nations were undated and pre-date the post-2021 refugee arrivals
  • ³ Freedom House, Freedom in the World 2026, status per country, re-checked October 2026 (Free figures changed for Uzbek, Tamil, Gujarati, Hindi, Punjabi, Japanese, Bosniak and Thai)
