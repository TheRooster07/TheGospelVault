Vault 3 · New Testament

Colossians

Christ Is All — a twelve-part small group Bible study series through Paul’s letter to a church he had never seen, written against a teaching that said Jesus was not enough. Christ is supreme over creation, the Church and the cross, and believers are complete in Him. Each study includes the biblical text, a devotional, a full commentary, a self-study guide with Greek word study, and personal application.

Source: The Gospel Vault  ·  Editor: The Gospel Vault  ·  NASB95
