About

Why The Gospel Vault Exists

This site started with a simple conviction: that ordinary believers grow when they're given unhurried, scripture-rooted teaching — not shortcuts, not hype, just the Word opened up plainly and applied honestly.

What Shapes This Site

Grace-Centered, Scripture-Rooted

Assurance

Faith Alone in Christ Alone

Salvation is a settled gift received by faith, not a performance to maintain. Everything here is written to help you rest in that assurance rather than chase it.

Clarity

Plain-Language Teaching

Scripture is unpacked in everyday language — short reflections and studies you can actually finish, not academic essays you set aside.

Practice

Built for Everyday Faith

Every devotional and study is written to be lived, not just read — a few quiet minutes that shape the rest of your day.

About the Author

B. R. Crow

TheGospelVault.com is designed and maintained by B. R. Crow. I am a layman with two years of Bible college at Miami Christian College, and I have been blessed to have great pastors and professors as mentors along the way. Much of what you'll find on this site grew out of their teaching, including notes and files I've kept and studied over the years.

Is AI in use here? Yes, to some extent. For example, notes from more than 20 years ago have been updated into a standardized format. Outlining has been part of my life for as long as I can remember, in business and in teaching. Research was also central to my work in business, and it has proven just as valuable in preparing new materials and adding to existing ones.

In some cases, material has been drawn from my mentors. One example is Dr. C. Norman Sellers, ThD. In the study of Galatians, for instance, his sermon was transcribed into his outline to serve as a commentary. The study method built around that core is how I was taught to prepare for a study:

  • Devotional: essentially a rewriting of the passage in fresh words
  • Word study: essential for a deeper understanding of the text
  • Application: which is, after all, why we study and share

Please enjoy the site. As always, your insights and feedback are welcome! You can also follow us on Instagram at @iJohn.316.
