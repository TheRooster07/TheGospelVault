The Vault — Devotional Series

John: An Eyewitness Testimony

Twenty-one chapters, twenty-one days — a verse-by-verse walk through the Gospel of John. Each chapter includes a key verse, a short reflection, and questions to carry with you. Start at Chapter 1, or jump to whichever chapter you're studying.

Chapter 1 · John 1:1–51

The Word Became Flesh

The eternal Word steps into human history — full of grace and truth.

 Chapter 2 · John 2:1–25

Water Into Wine, Zeal for His Father's House

The first sign at Cana, and a temple cleared for true worship.

 Chapter 3 · John 3:1–36

Born Again, and the Love That Sent Him

Nicodemus by night, and the love that gave the world its Son.

 Chapter 4 · John 4:1–54

Living Water, and a Father's Growing Faith

A woman at a well, and a father whose faith grows step by step.

 Chapter 5 · John 5:1–47

From Death Into Life

Healing at the pool of Bethesda, and the authority behind it.

 Chapter 6 · John 6:1–71

Bread from Heaven, Words of Eternal Life

Five thousand fed, and a harder teaching that thins the crowd.

 Chapter 7 · John 7:1–52

Rivers of Living Water

A divided crowd, and an invitation to come and drink.

 Chapter 8 · John 8:1–59

The Truth That Sets You Free

Grace for the accused, and freedom found only in the Son.

 Chapter 9 · John 9:1–41

I Was Blind, But Now I See

A man born blind, and the simple testimony no argument can shake.

 Chapter 10 · John 10:1–42

No One Can Snatch Them Out of My Hand

The Good Shepherd, and the eternal security of His sheep.

 Chapter 11 · John 11:1–57

I Am the Resurrection and the Life

Lazarus raised, and a question every reader has to answer.

 Chapter 12 · John 12:1–50

A Grain of Wheat Must Fall

The road to the cross comes into view, and what it costs to follow.

 Chapter 13 · John 13:1–38

Loved to the End

A towel, a basin, and a new commandment to love one another.

 Chapter 14 · John 14:1–31

The Way, the Truth, and the Life

A troubled heart calmed, and the only way to the Father.

 Chapter 15 · John 15:1–27

Abiding in the Vine

Fruitfulness that flows from staying connected to Christ.

 Chapter 16 · John 16:1–33

I Have Overcome the World

Grief turned to joy, and peace that doesn't depend on circumstances.

 Chapter 17 · John 17:1–26

The Prayer Jesus Prayed for You

Jesus prays for His disciples — and for everyone who would believe through them.

 Chapter 18 · John 18:1–40

Of Those You Gave Me, I Have Lost None

Betrayal, arrest, and a promise kept even in the garden.

 Chapter 19 · John 19:1–42

It Is Finished

The cross, and the completed work of redemption.

 Chapter 20 · John 20:1–31

That You May Believe

The empty tomb, and the reason John wrote this Gospel at all.

 Chapter 21 · John 21:1–25

Feed My Sheep

Breakfast on the shore, and Peter fully restored.
