The Lame Man at Bethesda

He Is Equal with God  ·  John 5  ·  The Seven Signs

Key Verse

“He not only was breaking the Sabbath, but also was calling God His own Father, making Himself equal with God.”

John 5:18

For thirty-eight years a man lay beside a pool, waiting for a chance he could never reach. When Jesus asks if he wants to be well, he doesn’t even answer the question. He explains why no one will help him. He shows no faith, no knowledge of who Jesus is, and no claim on Him at all. Jesus simply says, “Get up, pick up your pallet and walk,” and he does. Afterward the man can’t even name his healer. This is grace in its purest form, given to someone who brought nothing to the moment. When the leaders object that it happened on the Sabbath, Jesus answers, “My Father is working until now, and I Myself am working.” They understood exactly what He claimed: equality with God. The One who healed the helpless man is the God who never stops working.

A Question to Carry

  1. What have you been waiting beside, hoping you could finally reach it, when Christ has already come to you?
