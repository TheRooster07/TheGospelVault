Ask Your Own

Ask a Question — Privately

Have a question about God, the Bible, or eternal life that isn’t answered here? Send it below. You don’t need to give your name.

  • Your question is never posted. It goes to a private inbox read only by The Gospel Vault. Nothing you send appears on this site.
  • No name or account needed. A reply is optional. If you want one, give an email address or any other way to reach you that you choose.
  • Your contact details are deleted after we reply. If your question helps us write a future answer, it will be reworded so no one could be identified.
  • If you live where faith is watched, please don’t use your real name, a work email, or details that identify you. A separate, private email address is safest.

Leave the reply box unchecked to ask anonymously. We read every question.
