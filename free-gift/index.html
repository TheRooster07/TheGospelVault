A Five-Panel Walkthrough

Eternal Life / Salvation: God's Free Gift

Ask anyone if salvation is a free gift, and they'll say yes. Ask what it takes to receive it, and the answers start multiplying. These five panels walk through what the Bible — especially the Gospel of John — actually says the condition is, and isn't.

The Question

Salvation is a free gift — so what do you actually have to do to receive it?

 Panel 2 of 5

Why John's Gospel

One Gospel states its purpose outright: "that you may believe."

 Panel 3 of 5

Believe

The one condition, pictured six ways: look, hear, enter, drink, come, receive.

 Panel 4 of 5

No Additions

No qualifiers on faith, and no repentance or surrender tacked on as extra conditions.

Assurance

You can know — not just hope — that you have eternal life.
