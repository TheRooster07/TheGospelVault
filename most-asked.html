Does Acts 2:38 mean I must repent and be baptized to be saved?

No. Eternal life is received by faith alone in Christ alone (John 3:16). Baptism is important, but it is not a condition for eternal life.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 3:16 (WEB)

Peter himself makes this clear. Speaking to Cornelius and his household, he said that everyone who believes in Jesus receives forgiveness of sins through His name, and while he was still speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on all who were listening (Acts 10:43–44). Forgiveness came by believing, before and without baptism. They had no time to be baptized first. For believers today, water baptism is a step of obedience and a public testimony that follows faith (Matthew 28:19). Paul said Christ sent him to preach the gospel, not to baptize (1 Corinthians 1:17). If you have believed in Jesus, you have eternal life now (John 5:24).

43 All the prophets testify about him, that through his name everyone who believes in him will receive remission of sins.” 44 While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell on all those who heard the word.

Acts 10:43-44 (WEB)

Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

Matthew 28:19 (WEB)

For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the Good News—not in wisdom of words, so that the cross of Christ wouldn’t be made void.

1 Corinthians 1:17 (WEB)

“Most certainly I tell you, he who hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life, and doesn’t come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life.

John 5:24 (WEB)

More Scripture

Peter said to them, “Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

Acts 2:38 (WEB)

What is the gospel?

The word gospel means good news. In its shortest form, the good news is this: Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again on the third day, just as the Old Testament Scriptures said He would (1 Corinthians 15:3–4).

3 For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, 4 that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,

1 Corinthians 15:3-4 (WEB)

Because of what Jesus did, God offers eternal life as a free gift. It is not paid for with good works, religious duties, or promises to do better. It is received by believing in Jesus. Jesus Himself promised that whoever believes in Him has eternal life (John 3:16; John 6:47).

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 3:16 (WEB)

Most certainly, I tell you, he who believes in me has eternal life.

John 6:47 (WEB)

Nothing needs to be added. The Bible calls eternal life a gift (Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8–9), and a gift is received, not earned. Good works matter in the Christian life, but they follow salvation. They are never the way to get it.

For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 6:23 (WEB)

8 for by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, 9 not of works, that no one would boast.

Ephesians 2:8-9 (WEB)

How can I know I have eternal life?

You can know. The apostle John wrote a whole letter so that those who believe in the Son of God may know that they have eternal life (1 John 5:13). God does not want His children living in fear and guessing.

These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, that you may know that you have eternal life, and that you may continue to believe in the name of the Son of God.

1 John 5:13 (WEB)

Your certainty rests on Jesus' promise, not on your feelings or your performance. Jesus said that the one who believes in Him has eternal life (John 6:47) and has already passed from death into life (John 5:24). Notice the word has. Eternal life is a present possession, not something you hope to earn later.

Most certainly, I tell you, he who believes in me has eternal life.

John 6:47 (WEB)

“Most certainly I tell you, he who hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life, and doesn’t come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life.

John 5:24 (WEB)

So the question to ask is simple: Do I believe what Jesus promised? If you have believed in Him for eternal life, you have it. You were eternally saved the moment you believed. Doubts may still come, but even when we are faithless, He remains faithful (2 Timothy 2:13). Your salvation does not rest on how strong your faith feels today. It rests on His promise, so when doubts come, go back to His promises instead of looking at yourself.

If we are faithless, he remains faithful; for he can’t deny himself.”

2 Timothy 2:13 (WEB)

Can a Christian lose their salvation?

No. Eternal life is a gift from God, and Jesus said that those who have it will never perish and that no one can take them out of His hand (John 10:28–29). The moment you believe, the Holy Spirit seals you as belonging to God (Ephesians 1:13–14).

28 I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand. 29 My Father who has given them to me is greater than all. No one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand.

John 10:28-29 (WEB)

13 In him you also, having heard the word of the truth, the Good News of your salvation—in whom, having also believed, you were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit, 14 who is a pledge of our inheritance, to the redemption of God’s own possession, to the praise of his glory.

Ephesians 1:13-14 (WEB)

This does not mean sin does not matter. A believer who lives in sin loses closeness with God, may face His loving discipline as a Father, and can lose rewards when Christ reviews believers' lives (1 Corinthians 3:15). But he does not lose eternal life.

If any man’s work is burned, he will suffer loss, but he himself will be saved, but as through fire.

1 Corinthians 3:15 (WEB)

Your security rests on God's faithfulness, not yours. Even when we are faithless, He remains faithful (2 Timothy 2:13). A gift that could be taken back would not be a gift at all.

If we are faithless, he remains faithful; for he can’t deny himself.”

2 Timothy 2:13 (WEB)

Romans 8:38-39 (WEB)

Do I have to repent of my sins to be saved?

No. Eternal life is received by faith alone in Christ alone. Believing in Jesus is the one condition He gives (John 3:16; Acts 16:31). The Gospel of John was written so that people would believe and have life (John 20:31). It uses the word believe about a hundred times, and it never uses the word repent at all.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 3:16 (WEB)

They said, “Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved, you and your household.”

Acts 16:31 (WEB)

but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.

John 20:31 (WEB)

The word repent is used in two ways, and neither one is an added condition for eternal life. In one sense it means a change of mind about Jesus and what He has done for you: moving from unbelief to belief. That change of mind is simply believing, not a separate step. It can also mean turning from sin. That kind of repentance has to do with a believer's fellowship with God and avoiding His discipline, not with receiving the gift.

So you do not have to clean up your life before coming to Jesus. Come as you are and believe His promise. Turning from sin matters greatly, but it belongs to walking in fellowship with God after you have eternal life. It is not the price of the gift.

More Scripture

One who believes in the Son has eternal life, but one who disobeys the Son won’t see life, but the wrath of God remains on him.”

John 3:36 (WEB)

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. Christ is the Greek form of the Hebrew word Messiah, which means the Anointed One: the King God promised through Moses and the prophets. The Gospel of John was written so that people would believe this about Jesus and have life in His name (John 20:31).

but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.

John 20:31 (WEB)

Jesus is both fully God and fully man. He existed before Abraham was born (John 8:58), became a man, lived without sin, died for our sins, and rose from the dead. The prophets wrote about Him hundreds of years before He was born, including His birth in Bethlehem (Micah 5:2) and His suffering in the place of others (Isaiah 53:5–6).

Jesus said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM.”

John 8:58 (WEB)

But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, being small among the clans of Judah, out of you one will come out to me who is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings out are from of old, from ancient times.

Micah 5:2 (WEB)

5 But he was pierced for our transgressions. He was crushed for our iniquities. The punishment that brought our peace was on him; and by his wounds we are healed. 6 All we like sheep have gone astray. Everyone has turned to his own way; and Yahweh has laid on him the iniquity of us all.

Isaiah 53:5-6 (WEB)

Jesus promised that whoever believes in Him has eternal life (John 11:25–26). That is why knowing who He is matters so much: He is the One who gives that life.

25 Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies. 26 Whoever lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”

John 11:25-26 (WEB)

More Scripture

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

John 1:1 (WEB)

Is Jesus God?

Yes. The Bible teaches that there is only one God (Deuteronomy 6:4), and that this one God exists eternally as three Persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Christians do not believe in three gods. The Bible calls the Father God, calls Jesus God (John 1:1; John 20:28), and calls the Holy Spirit God (Acts 5:3–4).

Hear, Israel: Yahweh is our God. Yahweh is one.

Deuteronomy 6:4 (WEB)

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

John 1:1 (WEB)

Thomas answered him, “My Lord and my God!”

John 20:28 (WEB)

3 But Peter said, “Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart to lie to the Holy Spirit and to keep back part of the price of the land? 4 While you kept it, didn’t it remain your own? After it was sold, wasn’t it in your power? How is it that you have conceived this thing in your heart? You haven’t lied to men, but to God.”

Acts 5:3-4 (WEB)

All three appear together at Jesus' baptism: the Son is baptized, the Spirit comes down like a dove, and the Father speaks from heaven (Matthew 3:16–17). This is a mystery we accept because God has revealed it, not a contradiction.

16 Jesus, when he was baptized, went up directly from the water: and behold, the heavens were opened to him. He saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming on him. 17 Behold, a voice out of the heavens said, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.”

Matthew 3:16-17 (WEB)

It matters for your salvation. Only someone who is fully God could pay the full price for sin. And only God Himself, the Holy Spirit living in every believer, could keep that believer secure forever.

Is Jesus the only way to God?

Jesus said so Himself: He is the way, the truth, and the life, and no one comes to the Father except through Him (John 14:6). His apostles taught the same thing: there is no other name by which people must be saved (Acts 4:12).

Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.

John 14:6 (WEB)

There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven that is given among men, by which we must be saved!”

Acts 4:12 (WEB)

This is not pride. It follows from who Jesus is. Sin separates every person from a holy God. Only someone without sin could take our penalty, and only Jesus, God who became a man, lived without sin, died in our place, and rose again to prove it (2 Corinthians 5:21).

For him who knew no sin he made to be sin on our behalf, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 Corinthians 5:21 (WEB)

Good works or sincerity in another religion cannot pay for sin. But the one way God has opened is open to everyone: whoever believes in Jesus has eternal life (John 3:16).

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 3:16 (WEB)

Why did Jesus have to die?

God is loving, and He is also perfectly just. A just judge cannot simply ignore wrongdoing, and the Bible says the penalty for sin is death (Romans 6:23). So sin could not just be overlooked. It had to be paid for.

For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 6:23 (WEB)

At the cross, God paid it Himself. Jesus, who never sinned, took the penalty for our sin so that we could be declared righteous before God (2 Corinthians 5:21). He truly died, was buried, and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). In this way God is both just and the One who declares righteous everyone who believes in Jesus (Romans 3:25–26).

For him who knew no sin he made to be sin on our behalf, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 Corinthians 5:21 (WEB)

3 For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, 4 that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,

1 Corinthians 15:3-4 (WEB)

25 whom God sent to be an atoning sacrifice through faith in his blood, for a demonstration of his righteousness through the passing over of prior sins, in God’s forbearance; 26 to demonstrate his righteousness at this present time, that he might himself be just and the justifier of him who has faith in Jesus.

Romans 3:25-26 (WEB)

The cross also shows how much God loves us: Christ died for us while we were still sinners (Romans 5:8). Because the debt has been paid in full, God can give eternal life as a free gift to anyone who believes in His Son.

But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.

Romans 5:8 (WEB)

Did Jesus really rise from the dead?

Yes. The resurrection is the center of the Christian faith. The apostle Paul said that if Christ has not been raised, our faith is empty (1 Corinthians 15:14).

If Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is in vain and your faith also is in vain.

1 Corinthians 15:14 (WEB)

The evidence is strong. The tomb was empty in the same city where Jesus was buried. Jesus was seen alive by many witnesses, including more than five hundred people at one time, and Paul wrote this while most of them were still alive to be asked (1 Corinthians 15:5–8). His frightened disciples became bold men willing to suffer and die for what they had seen. And the prophets had spoken of it beforehand (Psalm 16:10).

5 and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve. 6 Then he appeared to over five hundred brothers at once, most of whom remain until now, but some have also fallen asleep. 7 Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles, 8 and last of all, as to the child born at the wrong time, he appeared to me also.

1 Corinthians 15:5-8 (WEB)

For you will not leave my soul in Sheol, neither will you allow your holy one to see corruption.

Psalm 16:10 (WEB)

Because Jesus lives, His promises can be trusted. He said that whoever believes in Him will live even if he dies (John 11:25–26).

25 Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies. 26 Whoever lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”

Can I trust the Bible?

Yes, there are strong reasons to trust it. The Bible says that every Scripture is God-breathed (2 Timothy 3:16), written by men who were moved by the Holy Spirit (2 Peter 1:21).

Every Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness,

2 Timothy 3:16 (WEB)

For no prophecy ever came by the will of man, but holy men of God spoke, being moved by the Holy Spirit.

2 Peter 1:21 (WEB)

The evidence supports that claim. The New Testament survives in more than 5,800 ancient Greek manuscripts, some from within decades of the originals. That is far more than any other ancient book, and it lets scholars know what the original text said, so it has not been lost or secretly changed. Prophecies written centuries in advance came true in Jesus, such as His birthplace (Micah 5:2). And archaeology has again and again confirmed people and places the Bible names.

But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, being small among the clans of Judah, out of you one will come out to me who is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings out are from of old, from ancient times.

Micah 5:2 (WEB)

Trusting the Bible is not blind faith. It is reasonable trust in a book God has preserved. The best way to test it is to read it for yourself, starting with the Gospel of John.

Why do Christians suffer for their faith?

Jesus warned His followers that the world would hate them as it hated Him (John 15:18), and Paul wrote that all who want to live godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted (2 Timothy 3:12). If you are suffering for your faith, it does not mean God has left you. You are walking where Jesus walked.

If the world hates you, you know that it has hated me before it hated you.

John 15:18 (WEB)

Yes, and all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus will suffer persecution.

2 Timothy 3:12 (WEB)

Scripture also gives real comfort. Jesus promised great reward in heaven to those who are persecuted for His sake (Matthew 5:11–12). Trials refine faith the way fire refines gold (1 Peter 1:7). And nothing, not trouble, danger, or even death, can separate you from the love of God in Christ (Romans 8:38–39).

11 “Blessed are you when people reproach you, persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely, for my sake. 12 Rejoice, and be exceedingly glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For that is how they persecuted the prophets who were before you.

Matthew 5:11-12 (WEB)

that the proof of your faith, which is more precious than gold that perishes, even though it is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise, glory, and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ—

1 Peter 1:7 (WEB)

38 For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, 39 nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from God’s love which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 8:38-39 (WEB)

God does not ask you to pretend it does not hurt, and He does not ask you to be careless with your safety. Pray, stay close to His Word, and find trusted believers when you can. If you have believed in Jesus, your eternal life is secure, whatever happens here.
