Colossians · Study 03 · Leader / Learner Guide

Colossians 1:15–23 — Leader / Learner Guide

The Savior of the Gospel · a one-page guide for leaders and learners

Christ Is All — A Study of Colossians  ·  TheGospelVault.com  ·  @iJohn.316 (Instagram)

Download PDF Full Study 03

Big Idea and Context

Big idea: Christ is supreme in creation, in the Church, and in reconciliation; believers already reconciled are to continue steadfast so they may be presented holy and blameless.

Having named "the kingdom of His beloved Son" (1:13), Paul now tells who the Son is, answering a teaching that made Christ one spiritual power among many.

  • In creation: image of God, preeminent Firstborn, Creator of all, Sustainer of all.
  • In the Church: Head, firstborn from the dead, the fullness of God.
  • In the cross: reconciliation in His real body through death.

The Supremacy of Christ — Three Anchor Verses

VerseWhat it saysTeaching point
1:15–16"the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation. For by Him all things were created"Firstborn means rank; He created all things, angels included.
1:18"so that He Himself will come to have first place in everything"The goal of the passage: Christ first in everything.
1:22–23"He has now reconciled you … to present you … if indeed you continue"Reconciliation is done; the condition governs the presentation.

Fullness in Christ: the plērōma is not spread among angels; it dwells permanently in Him.

Atonement: redemption (sin-ward), reconciliation (man-ward), propitiation (God-ward).

Teaching Flow

  1. The picture and priority of God (1:15) — Image of the invisible God; Firstborn = preeminence, not origin.
  2. Creator and Sustainer (1:16–17) — All things by, through, and for Him; in Him all things hold together.
  3. Head and Firstborn from the dead (1:18–19) — The Church's Head; first to rise never to die; all fullness dwells in Him.
  4. Reconciler (1:20–22) — Peace through the blood of the cross; reconciled in His fleshly body.
  5. The condition (1:23) — Continue steadfast to be presented holy and blameless; reconciliation is not in question.

Clarify While Teaching

  • "Firstborn": do not let it sound like Christ was created; v.16 rules that out.
  • "All things" reconciled (v.20): not universalism; only believers receive eternal life.
  • The "if" of 1:23: first class condition; it modifies "present," not "reconciled." Reformed readers often take it as a test of genuine faith; this study holds that it concerns presentation and reward.
  • "Proclaimed in all creation": either a deliberate overstatement or "all without distinction"; the study leaves both open.

Discussion and Close

  1. How does verse 16 answer those who say "firstborn" means Christ was the first created being?
  2. Why would Paul emphasize that Christ created the angelic ranks?
  3. What is conditional in verses 22–23, and what is not?

Close: Next, Paul tells of his own sufferings and stewardship of the mystery: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" (1:24–29). "He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together."
