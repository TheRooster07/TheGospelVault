The Vault — Devotional Series

John — 7 Signs: That You May Believe

John chose seven miracles and called them signs, because each one points past itself to who Jesus is. “These have been written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing you may have life in His name” (John 20:31). Each devotional includes the key verse, a reflection, a question to carry, and the original Instagram slide.

  1. 01Water into WineJohn 2:11  ·  The Beginning of Signs · He Is the BridegroomAt a village wedding, the vessels of ritual washing overflow with the best wine.→
  2. 02The Nobleman’s SonJohn 4:50  ·  He Is True to His WordNo touch, no visit, no proof — a father takes Jesus at His word.→
  3. 03The Lame Man at BethesdaJohn 5:18  ·  He Is Equal with GodGrace in its purest form, given to a man who brought nothing to the moment.→
  4. 04Feeding the Five ThousandJohn 6:11  ·  He Is the Bread of LifeFive loaves, two fish, and a crowd whose only job was to sit and receive.→
  5. 05Walking on the WaterJohn 6:20  ·  He Is the I AMIn the storm, two simple words: “It is I.”→
  6. 06The Man Born BlindJohn 9:25  ·  He Is the Light of the WorldHe couldn’t win a debate — and didn’t need to. “I was blind, now I see.”→
  7. 07Raising LazarusJohn 11:40  ·  He Is the Resurrection and the LifeThe last and greatest sign points straight to His own empty tomb.→
