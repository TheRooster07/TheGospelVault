Turning Away So Soon

Galatians 1:6–10  ·  NASB95

Key Verse

“I marvel that you are turning away so soon from Him who called you in the grace of Christ, to a different gospel.”

Galatians 1:6

Paul is stunned — not angry first, but genuinely amazed. The word he uses for “turning away” is a military term for desertion, and it’s in a tense that means it’s happening right now, mid-retreat, voluntarily. These were people who had received Paul “as an angel of God” when he first brought them the gospel of grace. And within roughly a year, they’re abandoning it — not because the case against grace was compelling, but because a different message showed up wearing familiar religious clothing. Paul is careful to specify: it isn’t just a different version of the same gospel. It’s not a gospel at all. The moment works get added to grace, you don’t have a stricter gospel — you have a different message entirely.

Twice in two verses Paul pronounces the strongest word he has — “accursed” — on anyone who preaches a gospel other than the one of grace alone, even if it were Paul himself, even if it were an angel from heaven. That’s not rhetorical excess; it’s how seriously he takes the purity of this one thing. Then he asks the question underneath all religious compromise: “Do I now persuade men, or God? For if I still pleased men, I would not be a bondservant of Christ.” Every time truth gets softened to keep people comfortable, that question is being answered the wrong way.

Questions to Carry Into the Passage

  1. The Galatians deserted grace within about a year of receiving it, drawn by a message that looked religious but added works to faith. What subtle version of "gospel plus something" might be creeping into your own thinking?
  2. Paul says adding works doesn't make the gospel stricter — it makes it a different gospel entirely. Is there an area where you've quietly started treating your standing with God as something you maintain rather than something already settled?
  3. Paul asks whether he's trying to please people or God. Where in your own life is the pressure to please people currently pulling you away from saying or believing something true?
