Charts for Philemon

Chart decks for leaders teaching the three-part Philemon series — one deck for each study. Download each as a PDF to read or print, or as an editable PowerPoint to teach from. Every slide carries leader notes.

Which Deck Should I Use?

For a group meeting, use that week’s study deck. Each one opens with a cover and a map of where the study sits in the letter, walks through the charts in the order of the commentary’s outline, and closes with discussion questions, applications and a one-line summary from the study. Every chart stays within the commentary posted for its study, with Greek words drawn from the study’s Greek word study.
