Vault 3 · Bible Studies

1 Thessalonians

A ten-part small group Bible study series through Paul's first letter to the Thessalonians — each study includes the biblical text, a devotional, a full commentary, a self-study guide with Greek word study, and personal application.

Ten Studies

Choose a Passage

1:1–5

An Introduction to the Thessalonian Church

Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy greet a young church already marked by faith, love, and hope.

 1:6–10

The Many Faces of the Gospel

The gospel in person, in proclamation, and in prospect — a transformed life, a spreading testimony, and a certain hope.

 2:1–12

Delivering the Gospel

Paul's own conduct as a messenger — no flattery, no greed, no glory-seeking, only a father's care.

 2:13–20

A Successful Life of Service

The word received as the word of God, suffering imitated well, and a hope that Paul calls his crown.

 3:1–13

Facing Tribulation

Paul sends Timothy to a suffering church, and finds his own life bound up with their standing firm.

 4:1–12

A Life That Pleases God

Sanctification, brotherly love, and a quiet, faithful life — the shape of pleasing God day to day.

 4:13–18

The Rapture of the Church

Grief with hope — Christ descends, the dead in Christ rise first, and the church is caught up together.

 5:1–11

The Coming of the Lord: A Purifying Hope

The day of the Lord comes like a thief for the world, but believers are children of the light.

 5:12–22

The Coming of the Lord: Exhortations

Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in everything — the will of God for an ordinary week.

 5:23–28

'Til We Meet Again

Paul's closing prayer, blessing, and charge — sanctified wholly, kept blameless, until He comes.
“Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.”
