Colossians · Study 06 · Leader / Learner Guide

Colossians 2:8–15 — Leader / Learner Guide

Beware of Philosophy · a one-page guide for leaders and learners

Christ Is All — A Study of Colossians  ·  TheGospelVault.com  ·  @iJohn.316 (Instagram)

Download PDF Full Study 06

Big Idea and Context

Big idea: Beware of any philosophy not according to Christ, because all the fullness of Deity dwells in Him bodily and believers are complete in Him.

The "persuasive argument" of 2:4 is now named: a philosophy built on human tradition and the world's basic principles, promoting rules, rites, and angelic mediators.

  • Beware: philosophy that is empty, man-made, worldly, and not according to Christ.
  • Be aware: Christ's full Deity, real humanity, our completeness, His headship over all powers.
  • Behold: spiritual circumcision, union with Christ, full forgiveness, the debt nailed to the cross, the powers defeated.

Complete in Christ — Three Anchor Verses

VerseWhat it saysTeaching point
2:9–10"in Him all the fullness of Deity dwells in bodily form, and in Him you have been made complete"He is the fullness; you are made full. Nothing can be added.
2:13–14"having forgiven us all our transgressions, having canceled out the certificate of debt"All forgiven; the debt paid and nailed to the cross.
2:15"He had disarmed the rulers and authorities … having triumphed over them"A Roman triumph: defeated powers paraded in public.

Same root: plērōma (fullness) and peplērōmenoi (made complete).

Doctrine and conduct: correct doctrine does not guarantee right living, but heresy makes it impossible.

Teaching Flow

  1. Beware of philosophy (2:8) — Kidnapping by empty, man-made, worldly teaching not according to Christ.
  2. Be aware of Christ (2:9–10) — Deity, bodily humanity, our completeness, His headship.
  3. Circumcision and baptism (2:11–12) — Without hands; buried and raised with Him through faith.
  4. Forgiveness and the debt (2:13–14) — Dead, made alive, all forgiven; the IOU canceled and nailed.
  5. The powers defeated (2:15) — Disarmed, displayed, triumphed over.

Clarify While Teaching

  • Baptism (v.12): the study takes it as Spirit baptism pictured by water baptism; many interpreters see water baptism directly in view.
  • The Law "as a unit": a dispensational position; others distinguish moral and ceremonial law. Stress that believers live under the law of Christ, not lawlessness.
  • Charge posted on the cross: the custom is attested (John 19:19); present it as a vivid illustration, not a detail stated in v.14.
  • Subject of v.15: God is the subject ("through Him" = Christ); some versions read "in it" (the cross).

Discussion and Close

  1. What "one more thing" have you been tempted to think would make you complete before God?
  2. If believers are "made complete" in Christ, what does that mean for any teaching that offers something more?
  3. How does the certificate of debt nailed to the cross bring assurance?

Close: Next, Paul applies this: let no one judge you by rules or defraud you of your prize (2:16–23). "In Him you have been made complete."
